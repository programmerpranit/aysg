# Reference board: AYSG hero (viewed sources, 5 Nov 2026)

Design read: marketing narrative for sangh donors, UPI donors, youth volunteers and CSR teams. Mode: Persuade. Lane: warm editorial, real documentary photos. Dials: variance 7, motion 7, density 5, art direction 7.

| Source | Role | What it actually does | Taken / avoided |
|---|---|---|---|
| vantara.in/en | Legitimacy reference (team pick) | One restrained promise headline over one full-width image and one "Learn More" action; proof sits further down | Taken: one promise, proof as its own beat. Avoided: no-donate model, founder framing |
| alwayscare.org | Sub-brand | Service headline, Gurudev credit as a small label, counters showed "0+" when fetched | Taken: credit as eyebrow, Get help kept separate. Avoided: live counters that can fail, so figures here are static text that only animate to their exact final value |
| charitywater.org | Adjacent NGO | Text-led opening, warm human photo, "impact to date" figures, tax status stated plainly | Taken: dated impact label, plain status lines |
| akshayapatra.org | Direct category, India | Carousel, stacked promo banners and a large menu compete in the first view; impact and certification come lower | Anti-reference for the first view. Taken: scale figures and certification as a distinct proof block |

Specific finding used: every one of these keeps proof in a separate beat from the promise. That is why the four acts are sequential rather than one crowded fold.

Asset cast: six real AYSG photos from scripts/images.json (pasti, anukampa, aahar, chaas, medical, education). No stock imagery. Not used: cupboard.jpg, its banner graphic competes with the headline.
