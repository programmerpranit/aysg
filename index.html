About AYSG

Giving has always been an act of self-discovery

Arham is an inner call to give up ego, overcome anger, practise self-control and live with passion.

AYSG started in Mulund, Mumbai, with 50 people. Today, inspired by Rashtrasant Param Gurudev Shree Namramuni Maharaj Saheb, 1,200+ members across around 60 centres bring that call into everyday seva.
